Mission Stament

Message: You should never be treated differently, judged, or excluded simply because you are different from others. Everyone has their own unique identity, background, beliefs, and experiences, and those differences should be respected and accepted rather than used as a reason to mistreat someone. Being different is not a weakness—it is a part of what makes each person unique.

Based on this our main goal was just to inform people that inequality is not good and needs to end.
